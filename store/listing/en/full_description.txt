Swap. Chain. Shine.

Swaplight is a fast, neon-drenched block-swapping puzzle game with a roguelite heart. Slide blocks left and right, line up three or more, and watch falling blocks set off chain reactions that send your score through the roof. Then take that skill into runs where every relic you pick bends the rules a little further.

No ads. No energy timers. No loot boxes. Just one game you can play anywhere, even offline.

THE CORE
• Drag a block sideways to swap it with its neighbour, even into empty space
• Match 3+ of a colour horizontally or vertically to clear them
• Clear 4+ at once for a COMBO, let falling blocks match again for a CHAIN
• The stack keeps rising: hold RAISE to bring up new rows when you are ready
• Every score is BASE × MULT, and the numbers climb fast

RUN MODE – THE ROGUELITE
• Three acts of stages with different goals: score targets, chain targets, clearing and survival
• A boss at the end of every act, each with its own curse: a hidden colour, a frozen column, a faster stack and more
• Earn Sparks and spend them in the shop between stages
• 40+ relics: passive power-ups that stack into absurd multipliers
• 15 one-shot charms: clear a column, freeze the stack, turn blocks into bombs
• 6 starting decks and 8 Brightness levels for when you want it harder

MORE WAYS TO PLAY
• ENDLESS – classic survival with ever-rising speed. How long can you last?
• VERSUS CPU – combos and chains drop garbage on your opponent. Five CPU levels, from relaxed to ruthless
• DAILY CHALLENGE – one board and a twist for everyone each day. Keep your streak alive and share your result
• PUZZLES – 120 puzzles in 4 packs: clear the board in a limited number of swaps. Hints when you need them
• TUTORIAL – learn swaps, combos, chains and raising step by step in a couple of minutes

MADE FOR TOUCH
• Precise drag-to-swap controls designed for one thumb
• Crisp vector neon visuals, particles and punchy screen shake
• Procedural music, and sound effects that climb in pitch with every chain link
• Colour-blind friendly: every colour has its own shape
• Reduced-motion, high-contrast and haptics options
• English and Hungarian

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
No account, no tracking, no analytics. Your progress is saved on your device and the game plays fully offline.
